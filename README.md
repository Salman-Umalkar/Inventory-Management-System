
# Inventory Management System

An Excel-based Inventory Management System designed to manage products, customers, vendors, purchases, sales, and stock levels. It includes a dashboard to monitor important business metrics and identify products that need reordering.

## Features

- **Dashboard:** Displays customers, products, purchase amount, sales amount, stock amount, and profit/loss.
- **Customers:** Maintains customer IDs, names, email addresses, and addresses.
- **Products:** Stores product codes, product names, product costs, and selling prices.
- **Vendors:** Maintains vendor names, contact numbers, addresses, and supplied products.
- **New Entry:** Provides navigation to purchase and sales entry options.
- **Purchase:** Tracks purchased products, vendors, dates, quantities, costs, and purchase amounts.
- **Sales:** Records customer details, products sold, dates, quantities, prices, and sales amounts.
- **Inventory:** Monitors purchased units, sold units, available stock, stock value, and reorder notifications.

## Dashboard Highlights

- Total customers and products
- Purchase and sales summaries
- Stock value and profit/loss overview
- Top 5 products
- Top 5 customers
- Stock quantity by product
- Low-stock and reorder notifications

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Excel Tables and Formulas
- Dashboard Charts and Data Visualization

## Project Objective

The objective of this project is to simplify inventory management by organizing business records, tracking stock movement, monitoring purchases and sales, and identifying products that require restocking.
